Curriculum Vitae

I am a Postdoctoral researcher at the MICS Lab at CentraleSupélec, where my research focuses on developing interpretability methods for foundation models trained on omics data. This work is carried out in collaboration with Prof. Wassila Ouerdane, the med-tech startup Scienta, and the Institut Pasteur.

I completed my PhD at the Collège de France as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow, under the supervision of Dr. Hervé Turlier and in collaboration with Dr. Maxence Ernoult from Google DeepMind.

My thesis, AI-based Methods for Inverse Problems in Biological Cell Systems, focused on developing computational frameworks that integrate machine learning and physics-based modeling for cell and tissue biology.

Before my PhD, I completed a Master’s Degree in Statistical Physics at Sapienza University of Rome and the Italian Institute of Technology, where I developed high-performance algorithms for shape matching and protein–receptor interaction studies. My Bachelor’s thesis, also at Sapienza, explored percolation in models with long-range interactions using analytical and numerical methods.

  • VertAX

    A differentiable vertex model for learning epithelial tissue mechanics

    The first fully differentiable vertex model of confluent epithelial tissues, built in JAX. Automatic differentiation, GPU acceleration and end-to-end bilevel optimisation make it possible to infer mechanical parameters from experimental data and to inverse-design tissue-scale behaviour.

    2026 · arXiv · Code · In preparation for Nature Computational Science

  • ZAugNet

    Self-supervised z-slice augmentation for 3D bio-imaging via knowledge distillation

    A fast, self-supervised deep-learning method that enhances axial resolution in 3D microscopy by interpolating between consecutive slices. Built in PyTorch, it combines adversarial learning with knowledge distillation.

    2025 · arXiv · Code · Under revision at Nature Communications
